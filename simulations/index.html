Simulations

A small collection of interactive simulations exploring how complex, emergent behavior arises from simple local rules. Everything runs in the browser on an HTML5 canvas — no backend, no dependencies. Click any tile to open the simulation.

Percolation

Randomly occupied cells on a lattice — does a connected cluster span the grid?

Forest Fire

Trees grow, lightning strikes, fire spreads. A classic self-organized critical system.

Abelian Sandpile

Grains topple when a pile gets too tall, cascading into fractal avalanches.

Diffusion

A random-walk / lattice diffusion model showing how concentration spreads out.

Conway's Life

The canonical cellular automaton — gliders, oscillators, and unbounded growth.
