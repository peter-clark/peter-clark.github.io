--- layout: default title: Simulations ---

A small collection of interactive simulations exploring how complex, emergent behavior arises from simple local rules. (under construction)

  • Percolation Randomly occupied cells on a lattice — does a connected cluster span the grid?
  • Forest Fire Trees grow, lightning strikes, fire spreads. A classic self-organized critical system.
  • Abelian Sandpile Grains topple when a pile gets too tall, cascading into fractal avalanches.
  • Diffusion A random-walk / lattice diffusion model showing how concentration spreads out.
  • Conway's Life The canonical cellular automaton — gliders, oscillators, and unbounded growth.
