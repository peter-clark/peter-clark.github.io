--- layout: simulation title: Abelian Sandpile sim_id: sandpile description: > Drop grains of sand onto a grid. When a cell accumulates four or more grains it topples, sending one grain to each neighbour — which may trigger further topples in a chain reaction. The Bak–Tang–Wiesenfeld model is the original example of self-organized criticality, producing scale-free avalanches and striking fractal patterns. ---
