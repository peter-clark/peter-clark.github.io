July 23, 2026

Tapping into Rhythm: How Polyphonic Drum Patterns Map to Monophonic Contours and Support Pattern Retrieval

Gómez-Marín, D., Clark, P., Jordà, S., Herrera, P. (2026). Music & Science (SAGE). DOI  ·  ORCID 0009-0002-1106-3894
  • Studied how listeners compress polyphonic rhythm into a single one-dimensional contour, and which rhythmic features drive it. Continuation of MSc thesis.
  • Built the force-sensitive capture system (Python, Pure Data); collected 4,128 taps from 43 participants. Mixed-effects modelling identified frequency-weighted onset density (FWOD) as the dominant predictor.
  • Trained neural regressors (PyTorch) mapping contours to coordinates in a 2-D similarity space of 1,513 drum patterns. FWOD best of three representations: R² = 0.79, Spearman ρ = 0.93, 89.6% localised within 10%.
